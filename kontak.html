Cara mempercepat penawaran How to get a faster quote

Semakin lengkap informasi di pesan pertama, semakin cepat kami bisa membalas dengan harga final. Idealnya sertakan: The more complete your first message, the faster we can come back with final pricing. Ideally include:

  • Kode artikel (mis. SQ1806-0405) dan jumlah per artikelArticle code (e.g. SQ1806-0405) and quantity per item
  • Kota tujuan pengiriman untuk estimasi ongkirDestination city so we can estimate shipping
  • Jenis kebutuhan — stok toko, proyek, atau pemakaian sendiriType of need — shop stock, project, or own use

Pertanyaan yang sering diajukanFrequently asked questions

Apakah produk TDM Electric di sini asli?Are the TDM Electric products here genuine?

Ya. Kami distributor resmi TDM Electric di Indonesia. Setiap produk dijual dalam kemasan original pabrik dengan kode artikel TDM (misalnya SQ1806-0405) yang bisa dicocokkan di tdme.ru.Yes. We are the official distributor of TDM Electric in Indonesia. Every product ships in original factory packaging carrying its TDM article code (for example SQ1806-0405), which you can check on tdme.ru.

Di mana saya bisa membeli produk TDM Electric?Where can I buy TDM Electric products?

Untuk pembelian satuan, belanja di toko resmi TDM Electric di Tokopedia atau Blibli agar transaksi Anda terlindungi. Untuk grosir dan proyek, hubungi kami langsung via WhatsApp.For single units, shop at the official TDM Electric store on Tokopedia or Blibli so your transaction is protected. For wholesale and projects, contact us directly on WhatsApp.

Apakah bisa beli grosir atau untuk proyek?Can I buy wholesale or for a project?

Bisa. Kirim kode artikel, jumlah per artikel, dan kota tujuan via WhatsApp, lalu kami balas dengan penawaran harga. Pembelian langsung dibayar via transfer bank setelah invoice kami kirim.Yes. Send the article codes, quantity per item and destination city on WhatsApp and we will reply with a quote. Direct purchases are paid by bank transfer once we have issued an invoice.

Pesanan dikirim dari mana?Where are orders shipped from?

Pesanan dikirim dari Jakarta Pusat. Pembelian di marketplace memakai kurir reguler yang tersedia di Tokopedia dan Blibli.Orders ship from Central Jakarta. Marketplace orders use the regular couriers available on Tokopedia and Blibli.

Apakah TDM Electric merek asal Rusia?Is TDM Electric a Russian brand?

Ya. TDM Electric adalah merek perlengkapan listrik asal Rusia yang berdiri sejak 2008, dengan katalog steker, stop kontak, saklar, sepatu kabel, fitting lampu, konektor antena, dan perkakas berisolasi.Yes. TDM Electric is a Russian electrical brand founded in 2008, covering plugs, sockets, switches, cable lugs, lampholders, antenna connectors and insulated tools.

TDM Electric's full range is far broader than what is listed here. Send the article code or a description on WhatsApp and we will check availability for the next shipment.
