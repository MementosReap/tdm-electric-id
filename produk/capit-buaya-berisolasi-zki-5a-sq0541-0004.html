Deskripsi ProdukProduct Description

Capit buaya berisolasi ZKI 5A dengan panjang klip 46 mm, untuk pengukuran dan sambungan sementara saat pengujian rangkaian. Selongsong PVC menutup bagian logam sehingga mengurangi risiko korsleting antar klip. Dikemas isi 10 pcs.ZKI 5A insulated alligator clips, 46 mm long, for measurements and temporary connections while testing circuits. A PVC sleeve covers the metal to reduce the risk of shorts between clips. Pack of 10.
